## Board, coordinates, and actions

- The lawn has five rows and nine columns. Plant trace fields reverse the input names: trace `r` is the input column and trace `c` is the input row.
- Cell centers are `x=630+122*col`, `y=130+138*row`. Zombie lanes use the same y formula.
- A valid scripted action selects its card at `F+4` and places at `F+15`. Selection deducts the cost and sets `hold`; placement clears `hold` and starts cooldown.
- An occupied-cell attempt is refunded at `F+15` and does not start cooldown.
- A click on an unavailable card has no effect.

## Economy and cards

- Initial bank: 10,000 sun. Costs: sunflower 50, peashooter 100, wallnut 50.
- Collected suns add 50.
- Sunflower and peashooter cooldown: 469 frames (7.5 seconds nominal), overlay decrement [ref] per frame. Wallnut cooldown: 1,875 frames (30 seconds), overlay decrement [ref].. The button becomes enabled one to four scheduler frames after `ready`; the simulator uses three.
- Startup readiness is special: sunflower is ready at frame 1 and enabled at 8; peashooter is ready/enabled at 470; wallnut at 1876.

## Plants

- Sunflower and peashooter HP: 300. Wallnut HP: 3,000.
- A common zombie bite removes 100 HP. Once a zombie reaches about 80 units right of the plant center it stops and eats.
- The stable single-zombie bite cadence is about 83 frames. Multiple zombies have independent animation phases, so interleaved plant HP changes can be much faster.
- A plant remains in the trace during its death animation after reaching zero HP. The simulator retains it for 251 frames.
- Sunflower first production occurs 250, 313, or 376 frames after placement. Repeats cluster from 1,406 to 1,656 frames; the simulator uses 1,469.
- Peashooters fire only when a shown, live zombie is ahead in their lane. Individual observed firing periods are roughly 62-90 frames. The simulator assigns a deterministic per-plant period in that range.

## Zombies and waves

- Common body HP: 200. Cone head shield: 600. Bucket head shield: 1,200. Body shield is zero for observed Level 4 types.
- Walking speed: 30 units/second, exactly 0.48 design units per frame. Zombies become shown at x about 1710.
- Spawn x is an inclusive uniform integer in 1780.2280, followed by the current frame's 0.48 movement. The engine is libstdc++ `default_random_engine` (`minstd_rand0`) seeded with the first high-32-bit SplitMix64 `random_device` draw.
- Damage is applied to head shield, then body shield, then body HP in steps of 20.
- Dead zombies retain `show=1` during their animation. Their reported speed falls to zero about 42 frames after death; removal is much later.
- Wave 1 spawns one common zombie in the top lane at frame 1139. The no-action second wave is frame 3226 with three commons in lanes 2, 3, 0.
- Player activity perturbs the original's shared random/scheduler state. Observed active-scenario second waves occur at frames 3139-3264 and contain three to five zombies. The simulator approximates this with frame 3201, three zombies without defenders and five with defenders.
- Wave 3 is near frame 5200. A later wave is forced after about 45 seconds if earlier zombies survive; if all are dead it can begin sooner. The penultimate warning wave can be followed six frames later by the large wave.
- The simulator uses deterministic lane/type templates plus a seeded engine for later cone/bucket choices. Exact lane/type generation was not fully recovered.

## Bullets

- Bullet speed: 666.667 units/second, about 10.667 per frame. The first scheduler frame advances only about 0.001.
- A hit is registered when the bullet is about 22 units left of a zombie. It sets `used=1` and deals 20. Used bullets continue visually before deletion.

## Suns

- Sky suns spawn at frames `floor(314+n*1562.5)`: 314, 1876, 3439, 5001, 6564, 8126. This is a 25-second cadence.
- At level construction, the game calls `srand(time(NULL))`.
- With no early plant, the first sky x is `500 + rand()%1100` from C-rand draw 52 (zero-based index 51), and horizontal drift is the next draw modulo 120 minus 60. A sunflower constructed before it consumes eight C-rand calls.
- An uncollected sky sun lands 314 frames after spawn and expires 1,312 frames after spawn.
- Sun collection disables the object, animates it toward the bank, and credits 50 after a distance-dependent delay. The simulator approximates that animation and delay.

## Mowers

- Five mowers appear during startup at frames 33, 39, 45, 51, 58. Idle state is `live=0, st=1, x=455`.
- A mower triggers when an alive zombie reaches x about 535 in its lane. Trigger state is `live=1, st=2`. After a two-frame delay it moves 12.16 units per frame (760 units/second), kills zombies in its lane regardless of shields, and disappears just after x 2100.

## Win/loss and trace termination

- Supplied public traces end at `end_frame` when no result occurs. The documented contract requires stopping 60 frames after a result overlay.
- Long idle measurement ended with program an abort before any `end=1` or `zwin=1` was traced. The exact no-mower loss threshold and overlay timing therefore remain unresolved. The simulator keeps `end=0,zwin=0` and writes through `end_frame`; this is the largest known gap for hidden loss scenarios.
