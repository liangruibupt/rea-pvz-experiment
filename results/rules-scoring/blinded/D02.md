## Clock, board, and startup

- One frame is exactly 0.016 seconds and frame 0 is emitted after the first game update.
- The board has five rows and nine columns. Plant records store input column in `r` and input row in `c`.
- Plant centers used by collision/economy logic are `x = 630 + 122*column`; zombie lane centers are `y = 130 + 138*row`. Pea bullets use `x = 700 + 122*column`, `y = 208 + 138*row`.
- The initial sun bank is 10,000.
- Card 0 becomes ready at frame 1 and enabled at frame 8. The mower rows appear progressively at frames 33, 39, 45, 51, and 58. The initial wave timer starts at frame 64.
- A mower begins at x=455, state 1, and is removed after crossing roughly x=2100. While running it has state 2 and advances about 12.067 design units per frame.

## Cards and plants

- Costs are sunflower 50, peashooter 100, and wallnut 50.
- Cooldowns are sunflower 7.5 s, peashooter 7.5 s, wallnut 30 s. The covered percentage falls linearly from 100 to 0.
- A successful card click charges immediately and sets `hold` four frames after the scheduled action. Placement occurs at action frame +15, clears `hold`, and starts cooldown.
- An occupied-cell attempt is treated as a cancelled placement and refunded. This is an assumption because the public traces contain no rejected placement; calibration scenario 03 motivates handling it explicitly.
- Sunflower and peashooter HP are 300; wallnut HP is 3000.
- A plant at zero HP remains in the trace for about 250 frames.

## Suns and economy

- A collected sun is worth 50.
- Sky suns first appear at frame 314, then every 1562/1563 frames (25 seconds).
- Uncollected sky suns arc briefly, fall at about 2.5 design units per frame, stop at a seed-dependent height, and expire after about 1312 frames.
- A newly placed sunflower first produces after about 5-6 seconds. Later production is seed-dependent at approximately 23.5, 24.5, or 25.5 seconds.
- Produced suns originate at `x=630+122*column`, `y=123+138*row`, then follow a short ballistic arc.
- The scripted collector examines the prior frame at each configured interval. Its click disables a sun three frames later; the bank increases 44 frames after that.
- Collected suns ease toward (290,1010) and are retained for several seconds. Exact visual animation and cleanup timing vary in the public traces; the implementation uses the measured central values.

## Zombies, waves, and combat

- Wave 1 releases at frame 1139 after an 18-second opening timer. Later releases are roughly 32-33 seconds apart, with a six-frame warning state.
- The observed first wave contains one common zombie in the top row. Later observed waves contain three or five zombies, seed dependent. The implementation uses one on wave 1 and three for even device seeds or five for odd device seeds.
- Public wave composition supports these early templates: even-seed wave 2 uses common zombies in rows 1,0,2; odd-seed wave 2 uses one cone then commons in rows 1,2,0,3,3; odd-seed wave 3 uses commons in rows 2,4,0,1,2. Later waves cycle rows and introduce stronger types.. Spawn x and wave-4+ type choices use the specified splitmix64 stream; later composition is approximate.
- Common zombies have body HP 200 and no shield; cones have body HP 200 and head shield 600. Buckets are assigned body HP 200 and head shield 1300 as a stated assumption. Evidence for common and cone: [scenario] spawn frames 1139 and 3201. No public bucket is present.
- All observed zombies move left at speed 30, exactly 0.48 units per frame. They become displayed around x=1710.
- Contact begins around plant center +80. Common first bite delay is about 50 frames and repeat period about 99; cone delay is about 41 and repeat period about 82. Every bite removes 100 HP.
- Peas travel right about 10.667 units per frame and deal 20 damage, exhausting head shield before body HP.
- Public shooters at even column 2 use a roughly 70-frame cadence; the column-3 shooter uses roughly 89 frames.
- A zombie reaching about x=535 triggers its row mower. The mower kills zombies in its row, including shields. If a later zombie passes the left boundary without a mower, zombies win. Evidence for trigger/kill: [scenario] frames 4005 and 5949-5955. The un-mowered loss threshold is an assumption because no public trace contains a loss.
- Dead zombies stop after about 35 frames and remain for about 470 frames.
- The level is assumed to contain ten waves and to win after the final wave is dead. No public trace reaches a win; this is recorded as an assumption for long hidden runs.

## Randomness and known uncertainty

- `std::random_device` is implemented exactly as the specified splitmix64 high-32-bit stream. Range conversion uses deterministic multiply-high mapping.
- A portable glibc-compatible `rand()` sequence is seeded from `time_seed` for sun animation and production variation. The documentation exposes both entropy sources, but the three traces do not uniquely identify which subsystem consumes each draw.
- Wave release jitter uses a deterministic seed/wave rule fitted to the observed 32-33 second range. Spawn composition, later-wave progression, sky-sun coordinates, death animation timing, collection cleanup, and win/loss overlay timing remain approximate where the public evidence is insufficient.
