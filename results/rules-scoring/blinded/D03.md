# Reconstructed Level 4 rules

This document distinguishes directly measured rules from assumptions used where the three public traces do not identify a unique rule.

## Clock and board

- One update is 0.016 seconds and one output row is written after every update, beginning at frame 0.
- The lawn has five rows and nine columns. Zombie row centres are `y = 130 + 138*row`. Plant trace coordinates intentionally follow the program's reversed names: `r=column`, `c=row`.
- The trace starts with 10,000 sun.

## Cards and plants

| card | cost | recharge | health |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| sunflower | 50 | 7.5 s | 300 |
| peashooter | 100 | 7.5 s | 300 |
| wallnut | 50 | 30 s | 3000 |

- Selection is reflected four frames after an action's nominal frame and immediately deducts the cost and sets `hold`. Successful placement is reflected at `F+15`, clears `hold`, creates the plant, and starts recharge.
- A card is enabled only if recharged and affordable. Sunflower is initially ready at frame 1; its button and the mower objects finish level-start setup at frames 8 and 33 respectively. Peashooter and wallnut begin with full recharge.
- Assumption: an occupied/out-of-grid placement is rejected, clears the cursor, and refunds the cost. Public examples prove occupied cells are relevant but provide no trace for that case.
- A dead plant remains in the trace with zero health for 251 frames.

## Sun

- Each collected sun is worth 50. Collection disables it on mouse release and credits it 44 frames later.
- Falling sun first appears at frame 314 and then about every 25 seconds (1562-1563 frames), beginning above the viewport, arcing briefly, and falling to a seed-dependent lawn height.
- A sunflower first produces after roughly 5-6 seconds. Its later interval is stable for that plant and was observed between 23.5 and 25.5 seconds. Produced sun starts at `x=630+122*column`, `y=123+138*row`.
- Sun motion to the counter is an exponential approach to `(290,1010)` and snaps after about 32 frames. Collected objects are retained for about 430 frames from birth; uncollected falling objects for about 1312 frames.
- Falling/producer sun randomness is modeled with a splitmix64-seeded `mt19937`; its exact original mapping remains underdetermined by the three public traces.
- Zombie horizontal positions use a separate C++ `minstd_rand0` (`default_random_engine`) seeded by the first documented random-device draw and `uniform_int_distribution(1780,2280)`. This exactly predicts every public spawn X: seed 103 yields 1911 for wave 1, then 2071/1902/2024/1907/1854 and 1848/1999/1873/2067/2041 for waves 2 and 3.

## Waves and zombies

- Initial wave timing begins at frame 64. The first wave is released at frame 1139 after an 18-second display timer and contains one common zombie in the top row.
- Later releases are about 31.5-32.1 seconds apart, with a six-frame release transition and a short post-release delay before the timer resumes.
- Common, cone, and bucket body health is assumed to begin at 200. Cone head shield is 600; bucket head shield is assumed to be 1300.
- Walking speed is 30 design pixels/s. Zombies become displayed at about `x=1710`.
- A zombie stops at approximately 78.7 pixels to the right of the occupied plant centre and enters eating state. Common bites remove 100 health, first after about 50 frames and then about every 99 frames. Cone timing is observed as roughly 41 then 82 frames.
- Wave lane patterns and growth after wave 3 are assumptions extrapolated from the public wave records. The public observations are wave 2 lanes `{0,1,2}` for seed 102 and `{0,1,2,3,3}` for seed 103; wave 3 lanes are `{0,1,2,2,4}` for seed 103.

## Peas and combat

- A pea begins at `x=700+122*column`, `y=208+138*row`, travels right at about 666.667 pixels/s (10.667/frame), and deals 20 to body shield, then head shield, then body health.
- A used pea keeps moving; a pea is retained for 189 frames from birth.
- Shooter cadence is stable per plant; observed periods span 69-90 frames. Assumption: cadence is selected from that interval by the seeded engine.
- A normal projectile death remains for about 470 frames. A mower death remains for about 315 frames.

## Mowers and result

- Five mowers appear at frame 33 at `x=455`, state 1, not live. A zombie reaching about `x=535` starts that row's mower, sets it to state 2, and dies immediately.
- A running mower advances 12.16 pixels/frame after its trigger frame, kills zombies in its row, and is removed just beyond `x=2100`.
- Assumption: a later zombie reaching `x=350` in a row whose mower is gone causes zombie victory. Ten released waves are treated as Level 4's final wave; clearing the final wave causes plant victory. The public traces do not reach either level result.
