## Clock, board, and trace

- One frame is exactly 0.016 seconds and traces start at frame 0.
- The lawn has five lanes and nine columns. Zombie lane centres are y = 130 + 138*lane. Plant records intentionally use `r=column`, `c=row`.
- Plant design centres used for attacks/suns are x = 630 + 122*column. Produced-sun y is 123 + 138*row.

## Economy and cards

- Initial sun is 10,000. Costs are sunflower 50, peashooter 100, wallnut 50.
- Initial card state: sunflower becomes ready at frame 1 and enabled at frame 8; peashooter completes at frame 470; wallnut completes at frame 1876.
- Sunflower and peashooter recharge in 469 frames (about 7.5 s); wallnut recharges in 1,875 frames (30 s). Overlay percentage decreases linearly from 100 to 0.
- A valid scripted card press is reflected four frames after the scenario action frame: cost is paid, `hold` becomes the card index, and the displayed overlay is 100. The plant appears and `hold` clears at action frame + 15.
- Sunflower and peashooter have 300 HP; wallnut has 3,000 HP.
- A zombie bite removes 100 plant HP per completed bite animation.

## Waves and zombies

- Wave timing starts at frame 64. The first warning begins at timer 18 (frame 1133), and wave 1 is released at frame 1139. Later waves are about 32-33 seconds apart, with a six-frame warning state.
- Wave 1 contains one common zombie in the top lane. Later waves contain increasing randomized groups. This submission uses the scenario's specified splitmix64 `random_device` stream directly for group details.
- Common/cone/bucket zombies have 200 body HP and respectively 0/600/1300 head-shield HP. Common and cone values are measured at [scenario] frames 1139 and 3201; bucket shield is an assumption extrapolated from standard progression and is recorded as a known gap.
- Walking speed is 30 design units/s, or 0.48 per frame. A zombie becomes shown below x=1710.
- Peas deal 20 damage, first against head shield and then body HP.
- Dead zombies retain a visible death state briefly, stop moving after roughly 35 frames, and are removed later.

## Peashooters and bullets

- A peashooter only fires while a shown, living zombie is ahead in its lane. Its first shot follows an animation delay; shot periods vary by plant in the observed program. The simulator deterministically assigns periods in the observed 69-90 frame range.
- Bullet spawn is x = 700 + 122*column, y = 208 + 138*row. Bullets move right by 10.6667 units/frame (666.67 units/s).
- A hit occurs at about 23 units before zombie x; the bullet then has `used=1` but continues until off-screen.

## Suns

- Sky suns begin at frame 314 and recur every about 1,562-1,563 frames (25 s). They use a ballistic entrance and settle on the lawn.
- Sunflowers first produce after roughly 5-6 seconds and repeat after randomized intervals near 23-31 seconds.
- Every collected sun is worth 50. The scripted collector disables a chosen sun on release and payment arrives 44 frames later.
- Sun trajectories are deterministic ballistic approximations; exact random launch velocities and cleanup scheduling remain a known gap.

## Lawn mowers and end state

- Mowers are introduced one at a time at frames 33, 39, 45, 51, and 58, initially `tag=-1, live=0, st=1, x=455`.
- A mower triggers when a live zombie reaches about x=535, changes to `live=1, st=2`, moves right by about 12.16 units/frame, kills contacted zombies, and leaves after 137 frames.
- If a zombie passes the left edge after its lane's mower is gone, zombies win; the trace continues for 60 frames after the result. The exact original loss boundary and final-wave win count are assumptions because no public trace ends in a result. Evidence for the 60-frame rule: [ref].
