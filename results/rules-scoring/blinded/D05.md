## Clock and initialization

- One frame is exactly 0.016 seconds.
- The player starts with 10,000 sun and game speed 1.0.
- Lawn mowers are created in row order at frames 33, 39, 45, 51, and 58. Each starts at x=455, `live=0`, `st=1`, and tag -1.

## Cards and planting

| Card | Cost | HP | Cooldown |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| sunflower | 50 | 300 | 7.5 seconds / 469 trace frames |
| peashooter | 100 | 300 | 7.5 seconds / 469 trace frames |
| wallnut | 50 | 3000 | 30 seconds / 1875 trace frames |

- Sun is charged when the card is picked up, at action frame F+4. A valid plant appears and the cooldown begins at F+15.
- Plant trace fields intentionally follow the program's reversed names: `r` is column and `c` is row.
- Occupied and out-of-grid cells reject placement. This is treated as a failed placement without starting cooldown. This is an explicit assumption; the supplied public examples contain only successful placements.

## Wave state

- The initial wave timer starts at frame 64. The warning state changes at frame 1133 and wave 1 is released at frame 1139.
- Measured later release centers are approximately frames 3195, 5220, and 8014. The first two inter-wave waits are about 32 seconds and the next is about 45 seconds.
- Wave 1 is one common zombie in the top lane. Later observed waves contain 3-5 zombies. The simulator uses a deterministic seed-derived 3-5 count and measured lane-coverage patterns.. Exact later-wave random draw order remains inferred.
- Observed equipment is 600 cone HP and 1200 bucket HP over 200 body HP.

## Zombies and eating

- Lane centers are y = 130 + 138*row.
- Walking speed is 30 design units/s, or 0.480/frame. A zombie becomes displayed near x=1710.
- A zombie begins eating when its center reaches approximately 79 units right of a plant center. Plant centers are x = 631 + 122*column.
- Bites remove 100 plant HP. Attack periods vary by zombie; the implementation uses a deterministic 1.0-1.6 second approximation.
- A zero-HP plant remains in the trace for about 251 frames before removal.

## Peashooters and bullets

- Peas deal 20 damage. Damage is taken from head shield before body HP.
- Bullet spawn is 69 units right of the plant center and at y = 208 + 138*row. Bullet speed is approximately 666.667 units/s, or 10.667/frame.
- Observed firing periods were about 70 frames for the column-2 shooter and 90 frames for the column-3 shooter. The implementation preserves those measured periods and starts firing only when a displayed, living zombie is ahead in the same lane.
- Used bullets continue their visual flight and are removed after leaving the right side.

## Sun

- Sky sun first appears at frame 314, then every 24.992 seconds (alternating 1562/1563-frame gaps).
- A produced sun appears at x = plant center - 1 and y = 123 + 138*row.
- First sunflower production occurs after about 5-6 seconds. Later measured periods are 23.5, 24.5, or 25.5 seconds.
- A collected sun becomes disabled on mouse release, flies toward (290,1010), and credits 50 sun 44 frames later.
- Exact sky-sun x, target y, and launch velocity randomization are approximated from the time seed. The supplied seed measurement establish that sky sun depends on `time_seed`, while zombie placement depends on `device_seed`.

## Mowers and end state

- A mower triggers when a zombie reaches approximately x=535, changes to `live=1`, `st=2`, waits one frame, then moves right at 760 units/s (12.16/frame). It disappears 137 frames after triggering.
- A mower kills zombies in its lane immediately, zeroing body and head-shield HP.
- With no mower left in a lane, a zombie reaching the house is treated as a zombie win. The long idle measurement process aborted before emitting an end overlay, so exact loss-overlay timing is a known gap.
- The exact final-wave count and victory-overlay timing were not reached by the supplied examples or accepted experiments and remain a known gap.
