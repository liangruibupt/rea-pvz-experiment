## Clock, trace, and board

- A frame is 16 ms and frame 0 is emitted. Output is contiguous through `end_frame`, except that an unprotected zombie crossing the left boundary ends the trace before an invalid next frame.
- The lawn has five lanes at y = 130, 268, 406, 544, 682 and nine plant columns with logical centers x = 631 + 122*column.
- Plant trace fields intentionally expose program storage order: `r` is column and `c` is row.

## Economy, cards, and planting

- Starting sun is 10,000. Costs are sunflower 50, peashooter 100, wallnut 50.
- Startup readiness: sunflower frame 1, peashooter frame 470, wallnut frame 1876. The sunflower becomes enabled at frame 8.
- Sunflower and peashooter cooldowns are 7.5 seconds (468.75 frames); wallnut cooldown is 30 seconds (1875 frames). Cooldown overlay decreases linearly from 100 to 0.
- A card selection takes effect at action frame F+4: cost is deducted, `hold` becomes the card index, and the overlay reads 100 while held. Placement takes effect at F+15.
- Successful placement starts cooldown. Occupied-cell placement refunds the cost and leaves the card ready.
- Plant HP: sunflower 300, peashooter 300, wallnut 3000.

## Wave clock and spawning

- The integer wave timer advances on a 62.5-frame cadence, corresponding to one second at 16 ms. Its observed global tick frames begin 64, 127, 189, 252.
- The pre-wave interval is 18 seconds. `wdead` is set at frame 1133 and wave 1 is created at frame 1139.
- Measured later maximum intervals are 32-34 seconds after wave 1, 32-33 seconds after wave 2, and 45 seconds after wave 3. A cleared wave may advance earlier. Wave 4 is followed six frames later by the large fifth wave.
- Spawn counts measured for Level 4 are one zombie in wave 1, 3-5 in waves 2 and 3, 3-4 in wave 4, and 11-15 in the final wave. The simulator uses a deterministic seed mixer within these ranges; the exact original engine-seeding transform remains unrecovered.
- Wave 1 is in the top lane. Wave 2 populates lanes 0-3 before adding a possible duplicate.
- Common zombies have body HP 200; cones add head-shield HP 600; buckets add head-shield HP 1200; body-shield HP is 0 for these types.
- Recovered: the original wave uniform-integer helper to be a Park-Miller `minstd_rand` engine using multiplier 16807 and modulus 2147483647.. The wave assembler requests 1-3 zombies per configured entry and rejects spawn positions within distance 100 of an existing spawn.
- Each zombie constructor seeds a Park-Miller state from `std::random_device`; the supplied random-device stream is SplitMix64.. The precise object-construction draw index was not recovered, so spawn positions use a deterministic calibrated mixer rather than a seed-specific table.

## Zombie movement, eating, and death

- Walking speed is 30 design units/second, so x decreases by 0.48 each frame. Zombies become shown at x approximately 1710.
- A zombie stops at approximately plant-center x + 79, sets `eat=1`, and `spd=0`.
- Eating damage is 100 HP per bite. The first hit is about 42 frames after contact and later hits are about 83 frames apart.
- Plants remain trace-visible for roughly 250 frames after reaching 0 HP, while collision ceases immediately.
- Dead zombies retain a visible death animation, stop moving after roughly 42 frames, and are removed later. The simulator uses a 280-frame bounded cleanup approximation.

## Peashooters and bullets

- A peashooter only attacks a shown, live zombie ahead in its lane. The first shot follows target acquisition by about 87-93 frames; later shots are about 69 frames apart.
- Bullet origins are plant-center x + 69 and y = 208 + 138*row.
- Bullet speed is approximated as 600 design units/second (9.6/frame). A hit deals 20 HP, consuming head shield before body HP.

## Suns

- Falling suns start at frame 314 and repeat every 1562.5 frames (25 seconds): 314, 1876, 3439, 5001, 6564, 8126.
- An uncollected falling sun remains about 1312 frames.
- A collected sun is disabled on mouse release and credits 50 sun 44 frames later.
- A sunflower first produces after a measured 4-6 seconds, then repeats at a plant-specific measured interval from 22.5-26.5 seconds.
- Sun animation target positions depend on original random state. The simulator preserves observed cadence, clickability, flight-to-bank timing, and lifetime, but approximates exact random x/y targets.
- Recovered: production chooses one of two throw animations with `rand() & 1` and creates a Sun object.

## Lawn mowers and end behavior

- Five mowers appear one at a time at frames 33, 39, 45, 51, 58. Initial fields are `tag=-1`, `live=0`, `st=1`, `x=455`.
- A mower activates near zombie x=535, changes to `live=1`, `st=2`, kills intersecting zombies, and moves right at approximately 11.52 units/frame until removed beyond the lawn.
- Mower kills set body HP to 0 even when a head shield remains.
- With no mower, the supplied program's no-action run ended before writing a frame with negative zombie x and did not expose an end overlay in its last valid frame. The simulator therefore ends its trace at that boundary without inventing `end` or `zwin`.

## Known approximations

Exact original transformations for random spawn coordinates, wave subtype selection, random sun target positions, animation phase offsets, and some wave-callback delays were not fully recovered. The implementation uses deterministic, seed-driven rules constrained by measured ranges. It never embeds per-frame measurement data or dispatches the program.
