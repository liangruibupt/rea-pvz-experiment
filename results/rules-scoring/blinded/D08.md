# Level 4 simulator rules

This is a clean-room simulator based only on the supplied scenarios, schemas, and public traces. Values marked **approximation** are extrapolations where the public traces did not identify the original random or scheduling rule uniquely.

## Clock and level setup

- One frame is exactly 0.016 seconds.
- Trace frame 0 is emitted before the level-opening animation has completed. The five mowers appear on frames 33, 39, 45, 51, and 58. Wave timing starts on frame 64.
- Initial sun is 10000. All three public traces show this on frame 0.
- Initial card states are covered and disabled. Sunflower becomes internally ready on frame 1 and enabled on frame 8. Peashooter becomes ready on frame 470. Wallnut becomes ready on frame 1876.

## Cards and planting

- Card order is sunflower, peashooter, wallnut. Costs are 50, 100, and 50 sun respectively.
- Sunflower and peashooter recharge in 469 frames (about 7.5 seconds). Wallnut recharges in 1875 frames (30 seconds). Cooldown percentages fall linearly.
- A successful scripted card click is visible four frames after the action's nominal frame. It immediately deducts the cost and sets `hold`. Placement occurs on action frame + 15, clears `hold`, creates the plant, and starts cooldown.
- Occupied or off-grid placement is rejected and refunds the selection cost. **Approximation:** no supplied traced scenario exposes the occupied-cell result.
- Sunflower and peashooter have 300 HP; wallnut has 3000 HP.
- Dead plants remain trace-visible for 250 additional frames.

## Suns and economy

- Collected suns add 50.
- Sky suns first appear on frame 314, then every 1562.5 frames (25 seconds), alternating the rounded 1562/1563-frame interval.
- Sky suns start at y=1150, make a short arcing motion, then fall over about five seconds to a random resting height. Their x coordinate, small lateral arc displacement, and resting height use the documented splitmix64 device stream. **Approximation:** the public samples do not uniquely identify the original bounded-distribution adapter.
- A sunflower's produced sun starts at x=`630 + 122*column`, y=`123 + 138*row`, makes a 40-frame arc, and rests at the same y.
- The first production is about five seconds after planting on the bottom row and six seconds elsewhere. Subsequent observed periods vary by plant from about 23.5 to over 30 seconds; the simulator selects a stable period from row and seed, including alternating one-frame rounding on the bottom row. **Approximation:** the public samples do not uniquely reveal the original per-plant random-period adapter.
- The collector examines the preceding trace state every configured N frames. A qualifying sun becomes disabled three frames later, eases to (290,1010), and pays out 44 frames after selection.

## Waves and zombies

- The first wave is announced after 18 seconds: `wdead` on frame 1133 and spawn on frame 1139. The first wave has one common zombie in the top lane.
- Later wave intervals are approximately 32-33 seconds. `wdead` is set six frames before each spawn. The timer origin is 12 frames before spawn. **Approximation:** [scenario] and [scenario] differ by six frames for wave 2, so a small seed-based phase is used.
- Wave 2 has three zombies for even device seeds and five for odd device seeds. The five-zombie form begins with a cone zombie. Wave 3 has five common zombies. Later waves increase toward ten and introduce cone and bucket zombies. **Approximation:** wave 2 is evidenced by [scenario] (three common) and [scenario] (five including one cone); later composition is extrapolated.
- Common body HP is 200, cone head shield is 600, and the approximated bucket head shield is 1300. Peas deal 20 to head shield first, then body shield, then body.
- Zombies move left at 30 units/second and become shown at x about 1710.
- A zombie stops around 139 units to the right of a plant center, bites for 100 damage, and repeats every 99 frames.
- Dead zombies coast briefly, stop about 40 frames after death, and remain visible for about 315 frames.
- Peas travel at 2000/3 units/second (10.667 per frame), persist for 188 frames, and remain visible after being used.
- Peashooter cadence is about 69.5 frames in column 2 and 89.5 in column 3 in the supplied trace. **Approximation:** this likely reflects a hidden randomized animation period.

## Mowers and end state

- Mowers begin at x=455 with state 1 and `live=0`. A zombie reaching x about 535 starts that lane's mower and is killed.
- A running mower enters state 2, moves right by 12.16 units per frame after a one-frame setup delay, kills zombies it intersects, and is removed around x=2096.
- A zombie crossing the mower threshold after that row's mower has been consumed causes zombie victory and the end overlay; output continues for 60 frames. **Approximation:** the supplied public traces do not reach a loss.
- `ts` is always 1.0 and `shovel` is always 0 for this scenario format.
