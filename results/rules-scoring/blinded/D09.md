## Clock, startup, and trace lifecycle

- One update is 16 ms and trace frame `f` is end-of-frame state.
- The player starts with 10,000 sun and speed multiplier 1.000.
- Sunflower becomes ready at frame 1 and enabled at frame 8. Peashooter becomes ready/enabled at frame 470. Wallnut becomes ready/enabled at frame 1876.
- Mowers are inserted at frames 33, 39, 45, 51, and 58, initially `{tag:-1, live:0, st:1, x:455}`.
- The wave timer starts at frame 64.
- Output includes frame 0 and stops at the scenario end frame, or 60 frames after a simulated result. The 60-frame rule comes from [ref].

## Cards, economy, and placement

- Costs are sunflower 50, peashooter 100, wallnut 50.
- Sunflower and peashooter recharge over 469 frames (about 7.5 seconds). Wallnut recharges over 1875 frames (30 seconds). The overlay decreases linearly by 0.213333 percentage points per frame for short cooldowns and 0.053333 for wallnut.
- An accepted card click occurs on action frame + 4, deducts cost, sets `hold`, and displays 100% overlay while held. Lawn release occurs on action frame + 15.
- Successful placement clears `hold`, creates the plant, and starts cooldown. A click while unavailable does nothing. An occupied-cell release clears `hold`, refunds the selected card's cost, and leaves the card ready.
- `en` requires both readiness and affordability. The implementation models the small UI polling delay after cooldown conservatively.

## Plants

- Health: sunflower 300, peashooter 300, wallnut 3000.
- Plant trace coordinates preserve the program's reversed names: `r` is lawn column and `c` is lawn row.
- A common zombie bite removes 100 plant HP. Its first bite is about 50 frames after eating starts and later bites are about 99 frames apart.
- Cone eating uses a faster animation, with a first event around 41 frames and later events around 82 frames.
- A zombie begins eating when its x coordinate reaches approximately plant-center x + 79, then speed becomes zero.

## Peashooters and bullets

- Bullet origin is x=`700 + 122*column`; y=`208 + 138*row`.
- Bullets move right by 10.666667 design units per frame (666.667 units/second).
- Each pea removes 20 points, applying to head shield before body health.
- Bullets remain in the trace for about 189 frames from creation; `used` becomes 1 on collision but the visual continues moving.
- Emission phase varies by planted animation. Measured recurring periods used by the simulator are 63-90 frames depending on row/column, with first eligible emission after about 87-93 frames.. This is an approximation of animation callback phase rather than a recovered source constant.

## Zombies and randomness

- Common body HP is 200. Cone shield HP is 600. Bucket shield HP is 1200.
- Walking speed is 30 units/second, serialized as `spd:30`, which produces 0.480 units per frame. Eating and completed death animations use speed 0.
- Zombies become shown when x falls below 1710.
- Initial x is an integer in [1780,2280], followed by the current frame's 0.480 movement. The exact generator is libstdc++ `default_random_engine` (`minstd_rand0`) seeded by the first specified splitmix64 device draw, with one uniform draw per zombie.
- Lane selection uses another `default_random_engine` seeded directly with `time_seed`, uniform 0-4. Already-used lanes are rerolled until all five have appeared; the used set then clears, including across wave boundaries.
- Type selection uses an independent deterministic stream and Level 4 probabilities. The probability table is measured from [ref]: successive configured rows begin 100/0/0, 95/5/0, 90/10/0 and progress toward more cones/buckets. The exact original stream seed was not fully recovered; the simulator uses a measured-fit independent minstd stream.
- A pea-killed zombie becomes `alive:0` immediately, stops after roughly 42 frames, and remains for roughly 470 frames. Mower kills stop sooner and remain about 315 frames.

## Waves

- Level 4 has 10 configured frequencies; base populations are `[1,3,3,3,10,4,3,3,4,10]`, with a large-wave marker at wave 5.. Actual traces add a small seeded variation: waves 2-4 add 0-2 zombies and wave 5 adds roughly 3-5.
- First-wave threshold is 18 timer seconds. Wave state changes to dead/paused, then the wave releases about six frames later.
- Later ordinary thresholds cluster at 28-34 seconds. A congested pre-large-wave interval can extend to 45 seconds.
- `wt` is an integer-valued floating field and resets to 0 on release. `wbegin` marks timer activity; `wdead` is briefly 1 during the pre-release pause.
- The exact adaptive timer and wave-size random streams remain approximated. The simulator preserves measured ranges and early public schedules.

## Suns

- Sky suns first appear at frame 314 and then every 1562-1563 frames (25 seconds).
- A sky sun begins at y=1150, follows a short rise/fall animation, then falls about 2.56 units per frame to a seeded resting y.
- A sunflower-produced sun starts at x=`630 + 122*column`, y=`123 + 138*row`.
- First production occurs roughly 5-6 seconds after planting. Recurrence is roughly 23.5-25.5 seconds, seeded/per-plant.
- Sun value is 50. Collection disables the sun on mouse release (collection frame + 3), credits 44 frames after release, animates toward (290,1010), and removes roughly 388 frames after release.
- The exact sky horizontal-position generator and jump arc are approximated. This is the largest measured public subsystem gap.

## Mowers and end state

- A mower triggers when an alive zombie crosses x≈535 in its row. On the trigger frame it becomes `{live:1, st:2}` and kills the zombie; it then moves right about 12.16 units per frame.
- A running mower kills zombies it reaches and is removed after passing x≈2100, reducing the `cars` list length.
- `UserLose` is 430 in Level 4 configuration. The simulator declares zombie victory when a zombie reaches x≤430 in a row whose mower is exhausted, then emits 60 additional frames.. Exact overlay timing was not isolated from a clean measurement loss and remains a known gap.
