## Clock and trace

- A frame is 16 ms and frame 0 is emitted. The simulator emits every integer frame through `end_frame`, unless a result occurred 60 frames earlier.
- The integer game-second transition is modeled as [ref], clamped to zero.
- `ts` is always 1.0 and the shovel is never held.

## Cards and planting

- Costs are sunflower 50, peashooter 100, and wallnut 50 sun.
- Initial sun is 10000.
- Sunflower is ready at frame 1 and enabled at frame 8. Peashooter is ready/enabled at frame 470. Wallnut is ready/enabled at frame 1876.
- Sunflower and peashooter cooldowns last 469 frames; wallnut cooldown lasts 1875 frames. Their overlay falls linearly from 100 to 0.
- A successful card selection occurs at action frame `F+4`; it deducts the cost and sets `hold`. A successful plant appears at `F+15`, clears `hold`, and starts cooldown.
- An occupied-cell placement clears `hold`, refunds its selection cost, creates no plant, and does not start cooldown.
- Plant HP is 300 for sunflower and peashooter, 3000 for wallnut.
- Trace plant fields intentionally follow the program's reversed naming: `r` is the requested column and `c` is the requested row.

## Suns and economy

- A sky sun first appears at frame 314 and thereafter about every 1562 frames (25 seconds). It starts at y=1150.001, animates upward, falls to a random lawn point, and expires after about 1312 frames when uncollected. Its random positions depend on `time_seed`, not `device_seed`: changing only device seed 101 to 999 preserved x=1510 and x=881, while changing only the time seed changed them to x=1234 and x=1416.
- A sunflower emits its first sun roughly 250--376 frames after placement, then at a randomized interval observed between 1406 and 1656 frames. Produced suns start at plant x minus 1 and lane y minus 7.
- A collected sun awards 50. The simulator marks the first eligible sun disabled at the scenario collection cadence, animates it toward (290,1010), and awards after 47 frames.. The exact original flight duration varies with starting position, so 47 is an approximation.

## Waves and zombies

- The wave system starts at frame 64. First-wave handoff begins at frame 1133 and wave 1 is released at 1139. Wave 1 contains one common zombie in row 4.
- Level 4 has five waves. Wave 4 is followed six frames later by the large final wave 5.
- Normal release gaps are about 27--45 seconds and depend on the random stream/game state. Several measured Level-4 table variants are selected deterministically from the documented device entropy.. This variant selection is an approximation because no supplied material exposed the original entropy-to-table transformation.
- Common zombies have 200 body HP. Cone zombies have 200 body HP plus 600 head-shield HP. Bucket zombies have 200 body HP plus 1300 head-shield HP.
- Zombies move left at 30 design units/second, or 0.48 per frame, and become `show=1` at x about 1710.
- Zombie spawn x lies in approximately 1800--2280. The simulator uses the documented splitmix64 stream and an inclusive integer mapping.. The exact original mapping remains an approximation.
- A zombie begins eating when it reaches about 79 units to the right of a plant center. It stops moving while eating and deals 100 HP every 84--85 frames.
- Grid plant centers are x=`631+122*column`; zombie lane y values are `[130,268,406,544,682]`.

## Peashooters and bullets

- A pea deals 20 damage, removing shield HP before body HP.
- Bullets start at plant-center x plus 69 and lane y plus 78.
- A bullet advances by 0.001 on its first update and then by 10.666667 per frame. It remains visible after impact with `used=1` and is removed beyond x=3060.
- Observed per-plant firing periods are stable and range from 62.5 to 89.3 frames (animation rates 10 through 7). The simulator uses a deterministic interval in that range.. Exact animation-rate selection is an approximation.

## Lawn mowers and end states

- Five mowers appear progressively at frames 33, 39, 45, 51, and 58. Their resting trace is `tag=-1, live=0, st=1, x=455`.
- A mower triggers when a live zombie reaches x about 535. It becomes `live=1, st=2`, advances by 0.001 on its first update and then 12.16 per frame, kills zombies in its row, and disappears beyond x about 2100.
- A living zombie crossing x=380 in a row whose mower has gone is treated as a zombie win. The result trace lasts 60 more frames. Evidence basis: task/schema end contract and observed mower breach geometry. The exact original loss threshold was not isolated by an accepted long-idle measurement, so this is a known approximation.
- Dead zombies keep moving briefly during their death animation, then report speed 0 and remain in the trace for a cleanup interval. The simulator uses 36--41 moving frames and 315--470 total frames depending on mower versus projectile death.
